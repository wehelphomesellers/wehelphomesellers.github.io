The Foreclosure Timeline

Understanding where you are in the process is critical. The window to act shrinks fast — and so do your options.

Day 1
Most Options Available

Missed Payment / Early Default

You've missed one or more mortgage payments. The lender has begun contact. At this stage, you have the most time and the most options — including loan modification, forbearance, a cash sale, or an MLS listing with maximum value.

30–90
Days
Act Quickly

Notice of Default (NOD) Filed

The lender has officially filed a Notice of Default with the county. This is a public record. You still have time to sell, negotiate, or explore alternatives — but the clock is running and your window is narrowing significantly.

90–120
Days
Urgent

Notice of Trustee Sale Posted

A sale date has been set — typically 21 days from posting. Options are now limited. A fast cash sale is often the most viable path to avoid auction and protect any remaining equity in your home.

Auction
Day
Last Chance

Trustee Sale / Auction

Your home goes to public auction. At this point, options are extremely limited. In some cases we can still intervene — but don't wait until here. Contact us the moment you receive any foreclosure notice.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what California homeowners ask us most often.

Is your cash offer really free with no obligation? +
Absolutely. Our consultation, property evaluation, and written offer are 100% free. There is no contract to sign, no obligation to accept, and no pressure of any kind. You can take as much time as you need to decide.
How fast can you actually close? +
For a cash sale, we can typically close in as few as 7–14 days from acceptance. This is possible because we pay in cash — there are no bank underwriting delays, no appraisal contingencies, and no lender approval required.
Do I need to make repairs before you buy? +
No. We buy properties in any condition — as-is. Whether your home needs minor cosmetic work or major structural repairs, you do not need to spend a single dollar fixing anything before closing. We handle all of it after purchase.
What if I've already received a Notice of Default? +
Contact us immediately. A Notice of Default means you are in the formal pre-foreclosure stage, but there is still time to act. We have helped many homeowners sell successfully after receiving an NOD. The earlier you call, the more options you have.
Can you help with a property that has liens or back taxes? +
Yes — this is one of our specialties. Liens, delinquent property taxes, HOA arrears, and similar encumbrances are situations we navigate regularly. In many cases we can purchase the property and handle the resolution of these issues at closing.
What if I want maximum value, not a quick sale? +
That's exactly what Option 2 is for. If your timeline allows, we can list your home on the MLS with professional staging, photography, and expert negotiation to achieve top market value. We'll walk you through a detailed estimate of what this could mean for your specific property.
Are you a licensed real estate agent? +
Yes. Sean Petersen holds California DRE License #02335346. Petersen Enterprises operates under Skyview Realty Inc., located at 10080 North Wolfe Suite SW3 200, Cupertino, CA. You are working with a fully licensed, regulated real estate professional.
What areas of California do you serve? +
We serve homeowners throughout California, with a primary focus on the Bay Area and surrounding regions. If you have received one of our letters, your property is absolutely within our service area. Contact us with your address and we will confirm immediately.
